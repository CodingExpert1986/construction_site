Privacy Notice

This static portfolio demo has no backend and does not transmit form submissions to Stonewise Construction.

Forms

Contact and FAQ forms only show a demo confirmation in your browser. Information entered in those forms is not sent to a server or saved by this website.

Demo Sign-In

The simulated sign-in stores a sign-in flag and email address in this browser's local storage. It does not verify credentials or store your password. Use the logout control to clear the demo session.

External Map

The Contact page embeds a Google Map. Loading that page may send a request to Google, subject to Google's privacy practices.
